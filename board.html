    Known limitation

    Published minutes are shown verbatim. There is currently no way to redact a specific passage before publishing — for example, to exclude in-camera discussions on legal matters, personnel, or sensitive contract negotiations. If your council regularly discusses matters that should not circulate to all owners, consider whether this portal is appropriate until per-section redaction is available.
