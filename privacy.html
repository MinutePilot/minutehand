Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

MinuteHand is operated by TOP SPIDER CANADA (CRA Business Number: 735810533) ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have. It applies to minutehand.ca and any related services.

We are subject to Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). If you have questions, contact us at hello@minutehand.ca.

1. What We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and an encrypted password, or the OAuth token issued when you sign in with Google. This is stored in our database (Supabase) for as long as your account is active.

Meeting content you submit

To generate minutes, you may submit one or more of the following:

  • Text notes or Word documents (.docx) — your text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a minutes draft, then returned to your browser. We do not store your notes or generated minutes in our database.
  • Audio recordings — your audio file is uploaded temporarily to our file storage (Supabase Storage) solely to obtain a URL we can pass to our transcription provider (Deepgram). The audio file is deleted immediately after transcription is complete. We do not retain audio recordings.

Generated minutes are returned to your browser and are not stored on our servers.

Payment and credit information

Purchases are processed by PayPal. We do not receive or store your credit card or banking details. We do store your credit balance and a record of credit purchases (date, amount, transaction ID) in our database.

Usage and technical data

Our infrastructure providers may log technical metadata such as request timestamps, IP addresses, and error events as part of normal system operation. We do not log the content of your meeting notes, audio, or generated minutes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To process your submitted content and return generated minutes
  • To track and deduct credits when you generate minutes
  • To process payments and issue purchase confirmations
  • To respond to support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your meeting content to train AI models. We do not use it for advertising or analytics. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with the following providers only to the extent necessary to operate the Service:

Provider Purpose Data shared
Supabase Database, authentication, file storage Account info, credit records, temporary audio files
Anthropic AI minutes generation (Claude API) Your meeting notes and text content
Deepgram Audio transcription URL of your audio file (Deepgram fetches and transcribes it)
PayPal Payment processing Payment details you provide directly to PayPal

Each provider operates under its own privacy policy and, where applicable, a data processing agreement. Anthropic's API terms state that API inputs are not used to train their models. Deepgram's API terms similarly restrict use of transcription data.

These providers may be located outside Canada, including in the United States. By using MinuteHand, you consent to your information being processed in those jurisdictions.

4. Meeting Attendees and Third-Party Personal Information

When you upload audio or notes from a meeting, that content may include personal information about people who are not MinuteHand users — for example, names, statements, or votes of meeting attendees. You are responsible for ensuring you have the necessary consents or authority to process and submit that information, in accordance with applicable privacy and recording-consent laws and your organization's governance requirements.

5. Data Retention

  • Audio files: deleted immediately after transcription
  • Meeting notes and generated minutes: not stored — processed in memory and returned to your browser
  • Account information: retained while your account is active; deleted upon request or account closure
  • Credit and transaction records: retained for a minimum of seven years as required by Canadian tax and accounting obligations

6. Cookies and Local Storage

MinuteHand uses browser local storage or session cookies to maintain your login session via Supabase Auth. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. No consent banner is required for session-only storage that is strictly necessary to operate the Service.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted credential storage, and access controls on our database. No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us at hello@minutehand.ca.

8. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and BC PIPA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent to the collection or use of your personal information (subject to legal and contractual obligations), which may require closing your account
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@minutehand.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

MinuteHand is not directed at persons under the age of majority in their province or territory. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
